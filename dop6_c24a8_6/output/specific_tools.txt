3. DIAGNOSTIC PCRs ACROSS THE PALINDROME JUNCTIONS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The palindrome is a 3.9 kb inverted repeat with a 1.2 kb loop: it will not amplify or clone
easily as one piece. Each junction is best tested with one primer in unique flanking DNA and
one in the spacer, giving a product that exists only if the arm is present and in that orientation.

  proximal (dop-6 side, arm A start)     junction at X:4324178
  armA -> spacer                         junction at X:4328106
  spacer -> armB                         junction at X:4329303
  distal (cst-2 side, arm B end)         junction at X:4333231

  Unique anchors available: X:<4,324,178 (dop-6 exons 1-9 side) and X:4,328,107-4,329,302
  (spacer) and X:>4,333,231 (cst-2 side). Arm-internal primers are ambiguous by construction.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4. CAN C24A8.6 BE DELETED WITHOUT TOUCHING dop-6?
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
C24A8.6 X:4330076-4332405 lies wholly inside arm B, so no
guide inside it is specific. The only specific cut sites flanking arm B are in the spacer
(X:4328107-4329302, unique) and distal to X:4333231.
BUT arm B overlaps cst-2 by 175 bp: cst-2 exon 9 runs X:4333057-4333346 and its 5' 175 bp are inside the arm.
So a spacer-to-distal deletion removes all of C24A8.6 and leaves dop-6 intact, but it also
truncates cst-2 unless the distal cut is placed inside the overlap and cst-2 is restored by HDR.

Practical consequence: the decisive experiment is not a C24A8.6 null. It is a dop-6 null made
in the unique 5' half (leaving both palindrome arms untouched) plus cDNA rescue. That assigns
the phenotype to dop-6 without ever having to separate the two copies.
