====================================================================================================
CONCLUSION
====================================================================================================
The pocket columns separate aminergic from muscarinic receptors easily (17 columns) and
separate DOPAMINE from SEROTONIN receptors NOT AT ALL (0 columns), although those ligands
differ far more than dopamine does from octopamine. The single DA-vs-OA column is above a
permutation null only because the null is 0, and one column is not a signature.

So: SEQUENCE AT THESE POSITIONS CANNOT ASSIGN DOP-6 A LIGAND. Both earlier claims are
withdrawn - the profile-similarity call (which read ancestry) and the 5.46 chemistry call
(which read family membership). MOD was right on both counts.

What survives is smaller and directional only: DOP-6 keeps 3.32 D and 5.42 S, the
adrenergic/catecholamine-family state consistent with D2-like descent; and it carries a
derived A at 5.46 that is unique among 163 tips and fixed across all five orthologues, so
something at that position changed and stayed changed. That is a fact about divergence, not
evidence about which amine it binds.
