TEST 3 - IS THE 5.46 / RING-HYDROXYL LINK ITSELF JUST PHYLOGENY?
====================================================================================================
Same objection applies recursively. If 5.46 state and ligand chemistry were each confined to
one clade, their correlation would be a single coincidence. Fitch parsimony counts how many
times each turns over on the tree; many independent changes means the correlation is
REPLICATED across lineages, which a profile-similarity score can never demonstrate.

  5.46=S agrees with ring-hydroxyl chemistry in 55/57 = 96 % of receptors
  5.46 S vs not          observed   8 changes, random mean  17.3, p=0.0005
  ligand has ring-OH     observed   6 changes, random mean  18.2, p=0.0005

  Both turn over 6-8 times independently, so the link is replicated, not a single-clade
  coincidence. This is the part of the analysis that is NOT a restatement of ancestry.

  DOP-6 5.46 = A  (dop-3 = S, dop-5 = S; all five dop-6 orthologues = A)

CONCLUSION. DOP-6 keeps the 3.32 amine anchor but has lost the 5.46 serine, which every one
of the 34 receptors in this panel that binds a ring-hydroxylated amine - dopamine,
noradrenaline, OCTOPAMINE and tyramine alike - retains. Octopamine carries a para-hydroxyl,
so this argues against octopamine on CHEMISTRY, by a route that does not go through ancestry.
It argues equally against dopamine, which is what the homology score wrongly favoured.
It does NOT say what DOP-6 binds; A at 5.46 is the indoleamine/ACh state.
