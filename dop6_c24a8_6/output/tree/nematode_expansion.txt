Reading (rewritten 2026-09-17 after referee audit - the previous text overreached twice):

  1. EVERY SPECIES IN THIS CLADE IS RHABDITINA. The clade column above says so. A clade with
     no non-nematode member is NOT the same as a nematode-wide radiation, and this one is
     restricted to Caenorhabditis + Pristionchus. Do not write "nematode-specific".
  2. THE ABSENCES ARE SAMPLING, NOT BIOLOGY. Real orthologues exist and are not in the panel:
     Cbr dop-3 = Q61H86 (1092 bits, and already used in dnds_vs_briggsae.tsv), Ppa dop-5 =
     A0A2A6CA79, plus clean RBH dop-6 in Necator (1110 bits) and Strongyloides and dop-5 in
     Haemonchus, all sitting unused in ~/data/proteomes/. The species table below is Compara
     coverage, not a survey. The non-Rhabditina nematodes in the panel returned no dop-3/5/6
     orthologue because reciprocal best hits cannot resolve this radiation - the retracted
     dating artefact - not because none exists.
  3. NO ANCESTRAL STATE WAS RECONSTRUCTED. "From a D2-like ancestor" was asserted from the
     sister relationship alone. Placement inside the D2-like radiation is supported; the
     ancestral ligand is not.

What survives: the characterised evidence near DOP-6 is thin - its first characterised
neighbour is dop-3, four genes out. Whether that is structural or a sampling failure is OPEN
until the sequences named above are placed phylogenetically.
