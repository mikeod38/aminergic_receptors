====================================================================================================
READING
====================================================================================================
"Predates the X split" means sequences from lineage X sit inside BOTH daughter clades, so the
duplication must be older than the divergence of X. It is a LOWER bound: absence of a lineage
inside a clade is not evidence the gene was absent there, because these proteomes are of
uneven quality and a real orthologue can simply not have been called.
