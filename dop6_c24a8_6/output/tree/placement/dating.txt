READING
====================================================================================================
A sequence from a lineage falling INSIDE a clade dates that clade to before the lineage split.
This is the claim a reciprocal-best-hit table cannot make, and the reason the previous dating
matrix was retracted: RBH sends a real orthologue to whichever paralogue scores highest, so a
gene that is present is recorded as absent.
