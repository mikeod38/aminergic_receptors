READING
------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The alpha5 helix of Galpha inserts into a cavity whose walls are TM3, TM5, TM6 and ICL2/ICL3.
A fragment starting at 406 brings TM6, TM7 and the tail, but not TM3 (with its D/ERY arginine),
not ICL2, and not TM5 — and without TM1-5 there is no cavity for alpha5 to enter, because the
cavity is formed by the outward swing of TM6 AWAY from TM3. Contact residues are necessary,
not sufficient: they only work assembled into the seven-helix bundle.
