RETRACTED 2026-09-17 (referee audit). The previous READING here said the clade at which
dop-3/dop-5/dop-6 first appear brackets the radiation. It does not, because the '-' marks above
are a METHOD ARTEFACT. Reciprocal best hits cannot resolve a recent paralogue radiation: in
Haemonchus one protein reciprocates with dop-5 at 976 bits while the reverse hits pile onto dop-4
and dop-1 indiscriminately, so every '-' means "reciprocated to a DIFFERENT PARALOGUE", not "no
orthologue exists". Confirmed counterexamples sitting unused in ~/data/proteomes/: Necator dop-6
(RBH 1110/1121 bits, 81.7%), Strongyloides dop-6, Haemonchus dop-5.

WHAT SURVIVES: the dop-2 column only - present in all six species and shared with Drosophila,
consistent with dop-2 being the ancestral lineage. Nothing here dates dop-3/dop-5/dop-6.

Dating this radiation requires phylogenetic placement of the candidate sequences, not best-hit
assignment. See reviews/2026-09-16_dop6_conclusions/DISPOSITION.md.
