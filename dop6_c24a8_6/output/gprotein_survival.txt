20/20 consensus Galpha-contact positions survive in the DOP-6 excerpt

Why: ICL3 contacts Galpha at its ENDS - where it leaves TM5 and where it enters TM6 - and
those ends are inside the HMM-aligned blocks, because they are the conserved helix boundaries
the model recognises. What gets excised is the middle of the loop, which is the part that
varies from 14 to 821 residues across this panel and makes no consensus contact.
